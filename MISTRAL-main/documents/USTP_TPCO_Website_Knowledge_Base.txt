COMMON NAVIGATION QUESTIONS:

Q: "Where can I find events?" or "Events?" or "Workshops?"
A: To access Events & Workshops, go to the "Resources" page by clicking "Resources" in the navigation bar. Then scroll down to the "Workshops & Events" section or click "View All Events" to see all upcoming and past events. You can also access events directly at /events. You can register for events directly on the page.

Q: "Where are the services?" or "What services do you offer?"
A: Click "Services" in the navigation bar to view all services offered by TPCO. The Services page includes core services (loaded dynamically from admin), additional services like IP Training & Workshops, Technology Assessment, and Market Research, as well as success stories from real partnerships.

Q: "How do I contact TPCO?" or "Contact information?"
A: Click "Contact Us" button in the navigation bar to access the Contact page. You can also call (088) 856-1738 Local 1145 or email ustp.tpco@ustp.edu.ph directly. The office is at USTP Cagayan de Oro Campus, Claro M. Recto Ave, Lapasan, Cagayan de Oro City.

Q: "Where can I see patents/technologies?" or "IP Portfolio?"
A: Click "Our IP" in the navigation bar to access the IP Portfolio page. There you can browse, search, and filter patents and technologies available for licensing. The page also includes analytics charts showing status distribution and patent types.

Q: "Where can I find templates/forms?" or "Download documents?"
A: Go to the "Resources" page and click on the "Templates" tab to find legal templates and documents available for download.

Q: "Where can I find guidelines?" or "Research guidelines?"
A: Go to the "Resources" page and click on the "Guidelines" tab to find research guidelines and best practices available for download.

Q: "How do I request a service?" or "Apply for service?"
A: Go to the "Services" page, find the service you need, and click "Request This Service" to fill out a service request form. You can also go directly to /service-request?service={service-slug}.

Q: "How do I apply for IP protection?" or "File a patent?"
A: Faculty members can log in through the Admin portal (/admin) and access the Faculty IP Portal (/faculty) to submit IP applications. The application process goes through internal review before being filed to IPOPHL.

Q: "What are the success stories?" or "Can you share examples of partnerships?"
A: Visit the Services page (/services) and scroll to the Success Stories section. There are 3 case studies: AgriTech Soil Enhancement (35% yield increase), Smart Construction Materials (20% cost reduction), and Food Preservation Innovation (50% shelf life extension).

Q: "How do I register for an event?"
A: Go to the Resources page and scroll to the "Workshops & Events" section, or go to /events. Click "Register Now" on any event with open registration and fill out the registration form with your details.

SECTION 8: MISSION, VISION, AND VALUES
================================================================================

MISSION:
Create and nurture a working innovation ecosystem that promotes entrepreneurship 
and commercialization of technologies to contribute to the economic and social 
development of the community it services.

VISION:
To be on the innovation map, visibly interfacing with stakeholders and creating 
a significant mark in fostering innovations not just in the academe but in the 
whole innovation community.

VALUES:
- Innovation: Fostering creativity and breakthrough thinking
- Integrity: Ethical practices in all partnerships
- Excellence: Commitment to quality and continuous improvement
- Collaboration: Building meaningful partnerships

ABOUT US (DETAILED):
The Technology Promotions and Commercialization Office (TPCO) serves as the interface 
between the talent and technology housed in the academic institute, the industry, and 
the community; fostering new relationships and partnerships resulting in life-changing 
innovations, enhanced productivity and ingenious discoveries that amends the human 
condition. The TPCO ambitiously aspires to be in the innovation map, visibly interfacing 
with stakeholders and creating a significant mark in fostering innovations not just in 
the academe but in the whole innovation community.

At the TPCO, we contribute towards nurturing an empowered innovation ecosystem that 
promotes entrepreneurship and commercialization of technologies to contribute to the 
economic and social development of the community it serves. As part of the university's 
commitment in delivering its Strategic Directional Areas, the TPCO was established and 
approved by the Board of Regents in May 2020 amidst the challenges of the COVID-19 
pandemic in the country. The office was officially launched to the public in August 2020 
and is strategically poised right at the forefront of the university making it accessible 
for stakeholders.
